Professional profile
Op. Dr Murat Üstün is a dual British-Turkish consultant surgeon with a career devoted to advanced laparoscopic and bariatric surgery. Trained in Turkey and practising internationally since 1996, he has performed over 6,000 advanced laparoscopic procedures, spanning sleeve gastrectomy, Roux-en-Y and mini gastric bypass, complex revisional surgery, and the full range of endoscopic bariatric therapies (Apollo Overstitch ESG, TORe, intragastric balloon).
He is the first and most experienced performer of Apollo Overstitch Endosleeve procedures in Turkey and the only Boston Scientific-certified surgical ESG trainer in the country. Currently practising at Liv Hospital Vadistanbul and the Memorial Hospitals Group, he leads international patient care pathways from London and Istanbul through Istanbul Bariatric Center.
Dr Üstün is a British citizen resident in London since 2016, actively engaged with the British Obesity & Metabolic Surgery Society (BOMSS) and the International Federation for the Surgery of Obesity (IFSO). His special interests include AI and digital health innovation, medical education, and patient-facing content in English and Turkish.
