What is ESG?

ESG is a non-surgical procedure that reduces stomach volume to help patients feel full sooner.

How it works:
	•	A flexible endoscope is inserted through the mouth
	•	Internal sutures are placed in the stomach
	•	Stomach capacity is reduced

👉 Results:
	•	Faster satiety
	•	Reduced appetite

Advantages:
	•	No incisions
	•	No scarring
	•	Short recovery period
