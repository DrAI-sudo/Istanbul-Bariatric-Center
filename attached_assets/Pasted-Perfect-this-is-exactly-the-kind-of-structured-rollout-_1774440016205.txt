What is Endoscopic Sleeve Gastroplasty (ESG)?

Endoscopic Sleeve Gastroplasty (ESG) is a cutting-edge, incision-free procedure that reduces the size of your stomach using an endoscope inserted through the mouth.

Unlike traditional bariatric surgery:
	•	No cuts are made on the abdomen
	•	No part of the stomach is removed
	•	Recovery is significantly faster

The procedure uses a specialized suturing system (such as OverStitch technology) to reshape the stomach, helping you feel full faster and eat less.
