How ESG Works

ESG reduces the stomach’s capacity through a non-surgical, endoscopic procedure, allowing patients to feel full faster and consume less food.

Procedure Overview:
	•	Conscious sedation ensures comfort
	•	A flexible endoscope is passed through the mouth
	•	Internal sutures reduce stomach volume
	•	Procedure duration: 60–90 minutes

Key Benefits:
	•	No surgical incisions or scars
	•	Short hospital stay (1–2 nights)
	•	Quick return to normal activities
